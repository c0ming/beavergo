隐私政策

Privacy Policy

适用于《小河狸大冒险》(BeaverGo) iOS / iPadOS 应用 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日

Applies to BeaverGo for iOS & iPadOS · Effective Date: November 26, 2026

核心承诺：不收集任何数据（Data Not Collected）
《小河狸大冒险》(BeaverGo) 是一款纯粹的离线单机游戏。我们不收集、不存储、不传输、也不向任何第三方共享你的任何个人身份信息、设备标识符或使用行为数据。

Summary: Data Not Collected
BeaverGo is a pure offline game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, analytics, or usage telemetry with ourselves or any third party.

一、我们收集哪些信息？

1. Information We Collect

我们不收集任何信息。本应用无需注册账号，不请求相机、麦克风、通讯录、照片库、定位、通知或广告追踪（IDFA / ATT）权限，也不集成任何第三方统计 SDK、广告 SDK 或社交 SDK。

We do not collect any information. BeaverGo does not require user registration, does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, notifications, or advertising identifier (IDFA / ATT), and does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

二、本地设备存储

2. Local On-Device Storage

为保证游戏正常运行，应用仅在你的本机系统存储空间（Apple UserDefaults）中保存以下非个人游戏偏好数据：

To save your game progress and preferences, BeaverGo stores only non-personal gameplay data locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults:

  • 已通关关卡进度、历史最佳步数与通关用时；Cleared stage progress, best step counts, and clear times;
  • 音效、背景音乐与触感反馈开关设置。Sound effects, background music, and haptic feedback toggles.

这些数据仅保留在你的设备本地，绝不会上传到开发者服务器；卸载应用即可将其一并清除。

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any external server. Deleting the app removes this local data.

三、App 内购买（StoreKit 2）

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

本应用提供「小雏菊篇完整版」与「彩蛋篇完整版」两项一次性买断内购，交易与「恢复购买」流程完全通过 Apple 官方 StoreKit 2 框架在本地与 Apple 服务器之间安全完成。

BeaverGo offers two optional one-time in-app purchases ("Full Daisy Pack" and "Full Easter Egg Pack"). All purchases and restorations are processed directly and securely by Apple through StoreKit 2.

开发者无法获取你的 Apple 账户信息、姓名、银行卡或账单地址。有关 Apple 如何处理交易数据，请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

We never see or receive your Apple Account details, name, payment method, or billing address. For details on how Apple handles payment information, please see the Apple Privacy Policy.

四、儿童隐私保护

4. Children's Privacy

《小河狸大冒险》年龄分级为 4+，适合全年龄段玩家。由于我们不收集任何用户的个人数据，因此同样不会收集儿童的任何个人信息，符合 COPPA、GDPR 及相关儿童隐私保护法规要求。

BeaverGo is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. Because we collect zero personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), in compliance with COPPA, GDPR, and applicable privacy laws.
