常见问题（FAQ）

Frequently Asked Questions

换了新手机或卸载重装后，如何恢复已购买的章节？ How do I restore my purchased chapters on a new device or after reinstalling?

请确保你的设备登录了购买时使用的同一个 Apple 账户。打开《小河狸大冒险》首页，点击进度卡片上的任意解锁标签或锁定关卡圆点弹出解锁窗口，然后点击底部的「恢复购买」按钮即可立即恢复你已买断的章节，不会产生任何重复扣费。

Make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Open BeaverGo, tap any unlock badge or locked stage dot on the Home progress card to open the unlock sheet, and tap Restore. Your purchased chapter(s) will unlock immediately at no extra charge.

游戏内包含哪些内购项目？是订阅制吗？ What in-app purchases are included? Are there any subscriptions?

没有订阅，也没有任何广告。游戏提供 2 个完全独立的一次性买断（Non-Consumable）章节包：

There are no subscriptions and no ads. BeaverGo offers two independent one-time (non-consumable) chapter packs:

  • 小雏菊篇完整版（com.beaverquest.ios.daisy_pack）：免费试玩前 5 关，买断后永久解锁全部 32 关。Full Daisy Pack (com.beaverquest.ios.daisy_pack): First 5 stages are free; one-time purchase permanently unlocks all 32 Daisy stages.
  • 彩蛋篇完整版（com.beaverquest.ios.egg_pack）：通关全部 32 关小雏菊篇后可免费试玩前 2 关，或随时直接买断立即解锁全部 24 关复活节彩蛋篇。Full Easter Egg Pack (com.beaverquest.ios.egg_pack): Clearing all 32 Daisy stages unlocks the first 2 Egg stages free, or you can purchase anytime for immediate access to all 24 Easter Egg stages.

注：两个章节包均不支持家人共享，仅限购买者的 Apple 账户使用。

Note: Family Sharing is not enabled for these packs; unlocks are tied to the purchasing Apple Account.

如何申请内购退款？ How can I request a refund for an in-app purchase?

所有付款与退款均由 Apple App Store 统一处理。你可以访问 Apple 官方退款入口 reportaproblem.apple.com，使用你的 Apple 账户登录后选择对应订单提交退款申请。

All billing and refunds are handled directly by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, and follow the prompts to request a refund.

如何切换视角、开关声音或重玩已通关的关卡？ How do I switch camera view, toggle sound, or replay cleared stages?
  • 切换视野：在游戏页面右下角点击「标准视野 / 广角视野」按钮，可在近景沉浸与纵览全局之间随时切换。Camera View: Tap the "Standard View / Wide View" button in the bottom-right control panel to switch between close-up and full-board view.
  • 音效与触感：点击首页右上角或游戏顶部栏的喇叭图标即可快速静音或开启声音。Audio & Haptics: Tap the speaker icon in the top-right corner of the Home or Game screen.
  • 重玩已过关卡：在首页进度卡片中，直接点击任意已点亮的金色关卡圆点即可重温并冲击更少步数纪录。Replay Cleared Stages: Tap any filled stage dot on the Home progress card to replay and beat your best move count.
需要联网或注册账号才能玩吗？ Does BeaverGo require an internet connection or an account?

不需要！游戏本身 100% 离线运行，无需登录或注册账号，所有关卡进度与最佳纪录均保存在本地设备中。仅在进行内购购买或恢复购买时需要联网连接 Apple App Store。

No! BeaverGo plays 100% offline with no login or account required. All stage progress and best records are stored locally on your device. An internet connection is only needed when purchasing or restoring an in-app chapter pack via Apple's App Store.
